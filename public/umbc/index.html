About

What is Banana Hacks?

Banana Hacks is a week-long virtual hackathon dedicated to generative AI and image creation. Whether you're building a novel diffusion pipeline, a creative prompt engine, or an AI-powered art tool — this is your quest.

Open to hackers of all skill levels. Work solo or form a team of up to four. Build something extraordinary in seven days and compete for glory and prizes.
